Flow A · Downstream master data

SAP B1 → LOC HOST SMS → LOC POS

SAP owns the item. Item code, UPC, description, category, UOM and tax are maintained only in SAP. LOC reads them and never writes back.

LOC owns the retail layer. Selling price, effective dates, discounts, promotions and tenders are set in LOC HOST SMS before going to the stores.

Rejects go back to the source. A record that fails validation is logged and corrected in SAP. Only failed records are resent.

Flow B · Upstream sales posting

LOC POS → LOC HOST SMS → B1iF → SAP B1

Full detail leaves the store. Every receipt carries store, terminal, UPC lines, gross, discount, net and tender, even when SAP posts it in summary.

Hybrid posting. Walk-in sales are consolidated per store, terminal and day. Charge sales post one by one against the customer account.

Nothing posts twice. Receipt number plus store, terminal and business date blocks duplicates. Daily totals are reconciled by store and tender.
